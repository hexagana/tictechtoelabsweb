Phase 1 · Open Copilot & pick the right model

Sign in, open Copilot, and switch from Auto to GPT 5.5 Think Deeper
4 steps
1

Navigate to M365 Office Portal and Log In

Open your browser and go to the Microsoft 365 Office Portal. Sign in with your work account. Once you reach the Copilot home, you should see the prompt box with “Hi, what can I help you with?” ready for input.

2

Click the “Auto” drop-down button

Look at the top-right corner of the Copilot interface. You'll see a button labelled Auto next to a small chip — that's the model selector. Click it to choose which AI model handles your request.

3

Click “GPT”

When the menu opens, you'll see several model options: Auto, Quick Response, Think Deeper, Opus, and GPT. Click GPT to expand its sub-menu of OpenAI models.

4

Click “GPT 5.5 Think Deeper”

Inside the GPT sub-menu, choose GPT 5.5 Think Deeper. The “Think Deeper” variant takes longer but reasons more carefully — which is exactly what you want when building structured outputs like a dashboard from raw data.

📊

Phase 2 · Generate the revenue dashboard

Upload your Excel data, paste the dashboard prompt, and download the result
5 steps
5

Upload your Excel raw data in the prompt box

Back in the prompt box, click the + icon (or drag and drop) to upload your Excel file. Copilot will show a small chip with the file name above the input area, confirming it's attached. Uploads are copied to your OneDrive automatically.

📥 File for this step XLSX Revenue_Jan-Apr_2026_Combined.xlsx 23.0 KB
6

Paste the dashboard-creation prompt

Now type (or paste) the dashboard prompt into the message box. This prompt explicitly asks for every section you want — total revenue, breakdown by class, breakdown by trainer, monthly breakdown, individual share, intelligent insights, outliers, and a forward projection. Being specific up front saves several rounds of revisions later.

Dashboard creation prompt 
Create a revenue dashboard from this data. The data needs to show a section of total revenue. The data needs to show the breakdown of revenue per class. The data needs to show a section of the breakdown of revenue per Trainer. The data needs to show a breakdown of revenue by months. The data needs to show a section of how much is Allan's share. Include intelligent insights about the data. Include a section to show any outliers. Include a section to project the revenue all the way until December 2026.
7

Click the submit icon

Once your prompt and file are both in place, click the blue circular → button on the right to send the request. Copilot will begin reasoning and building the dashboard.

8

Wait for the result to be generated

Copilot will show a “Coding and executing” trace as it runs Python in the background (pandas, numpy, openpyxl) to parse your Excel and build the dashboard. With Think Deeper selected, this can take 30 seconds to a couple of minutes — let it finish without interrupting.

9

Click “Download the Revenue Dashboard”

When generation completes, Copilot summarises what's in the dashboard (total revenue, breakdown by class, breakdown by trainer, breakdown by month, individual share) and provides a Download the Revenue Dashboard link. Click it to open the generated HTML dashboard in your browser.

Phase 3 · Refine the projection section

Iterate on the dashboard with a follow-up revision prompt
3 steps
10

Click “Message Copilot” to request a revision

Reviewing the dashboard, you spot something to refine — for example, the projection section needs adjusting for known seasonality. Click back into the Message Copilot input box at the bottom to ask for a revision without re-running everything from scratch.

11

Type the revision prompt and submit

Tell Copilot exactly what to change and why. The “why” matters — it gives the AI business context to make sensible numerical assumptions. Here we're asking for a seasonality-aware projection that accounts for a government skills-classes announcement driving higher uptake.

Revision prompt 
I need the projection section to be amended as we will expect more classes between August to October and low-volume classes in December. Ever since there was a big announcement by the government about digital skills classes, we expect a 10%-15% uptake of classes from May onwards.
12

Click “Download the revised dashboard here”

Copilot regenerates the projection with the new assumptions baked in — you'll see updated full-year, conservative, strong, Aug–Oct, and December revenue figures alongside a new projection chart, assumptions section, and monthly table. Click Download the revised dashboard here to open the updated HTML file.

💡 Tips for getting the most out of Copilot

  • Be specific in your first prompt. List every section you want — total, breakdowns, insights, outliers, projection. The clearer the request, the fewer revisions you'll need.
  • Use Think Deeper for structured outputs. Dashboards, reports, and analytical work benefit from the slower, more thorough reasoning mode. Save Quick Response for short Q&A.
  • Iterate, don't restart. When a section needs changing, just message Copilot in the same thread with the change and the why behind it. It will regenerate only what needs to change.
  • Give business context. Phrases like “we expect higher volume Aug–Oct due to a government announcement” give the AI the assumptions it needs to produce sensible numbers — not just averages.
  • Always open and verify the downloaded HTML. Open it in your browser, sanity-check the totals against your source spreadsheet, and only then share it.
